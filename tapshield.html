What is Tapjacking?

Tapjacking happens when a malicious app draws an invisible or disguised layer over another app's screen. You think you are tapping something safe — like "Deny" on a permission prompt — but the invisible layer secretly redirects your tap to something else, like "Allow."

  • Android reports every touch with flags showing whether another window was drawing over the app at that moment.
  • CyberShield checks this flag on every tap, in real time, with no extra permissions required.
  • If a tap is flagged, you'll get an instant warning and it's logged below.
