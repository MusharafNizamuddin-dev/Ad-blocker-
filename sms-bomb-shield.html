SMS Bomb Shield
Detect & respond to SMS flooding

When an attacker enters your number into signup forms across many different sites, each site sends you a real code from its own number. That flood is happening on their servers, so no app on your phone can stop the sending itself. What this tool does instead: watch for that exact pattern and give you a fast way to quiet your phone while you report it.

1. Detection runs in the background

Once turned on below, it watches for 4 or more different senders texting you within 3 minutes - the real signature of an SMS bombing attack.

2. You get an alert with a Silence button

Tapping it turns on Do Not Disturb for 15 minutes, so your phone stops buzzing. It turns itself back off automatically after that.

3. You report it to your carrier

Only your carrier can actually stop the messages at the source. Use the template below.

Turning detection on needs two one-time permissions in your phone's Settings app (this can't be done automatically):

• Notification access - Settings > Apps > Special app access > Notification access > turn on CyberShield
• Do Not Disturb access - Settings > Apps > Special app access > Do Not Disturb access > turn on CyberShield

The exact wording may differ slightly by phone brand, but it's always under Settings > Apps > Special app access.
