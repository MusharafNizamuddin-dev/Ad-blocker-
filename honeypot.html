Honeypot Vault
Bait files that catch snoopers in the act

Instead of guessing which permissions are dangerous, this plants fake "juicy" files — a bank backup, a crypto seed phrase, a password export — in your Downloads folder. They contain zero real data. No legitimate app has any reason to ever touch them, so if anything modifies, renames, or deletes one, that's a clean, low-noise signal something scanned your storage looking for exactly this kind of file.

Honest limits: this reliably catches a file being changed, renamed, or deleted — exactly what ransomware and cleanup-after-theft malware actually do. It can't catch a silent read with certainty; Android doesn't give any regular app a way to see "another app merely opened this file," so a scanner that only copies the bytes and leaves the file untouched won't trip this. Requires Android 10 or higher. Want to test it yourself? Deploy, then open your Files app and rename or delete one of the decoy files — come back here and tap "Check Now."
